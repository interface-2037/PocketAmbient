Ambient Pocket

Generative ambient music, indefinitely: DRIFT, TIDES, HUSH and MOTHER.
The composer runs on the Pocket (PicoRV32 soft CPU) and plays DRIFT, TIDES, HUSH and
MOTHER live. A starts / stops, Left / Right changes mode, B reseeds, Up / Down density,
L / R brightness, X cycles the visuals (Full / Minimal / Off), Y shows the info box.
Settings are also in the core menu.
Based on the openFPGA core template (https://github.com/open-fpga/core-template).
