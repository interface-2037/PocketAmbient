Ambient Pocket

Generative ambient music, indefinitely: DRIFT, TIDES, HUSH and MOTHER.
Phase 4 test build: the composer runs on the Pocket (PicoRV32 soft CPU) and plays
DRIFT, TIDES, HUSH and MOTHER live. A starts / stops, Left / Right changes mode,
B reseeds; Up/Down density, L/R brightness. Settings are also in the core menu.
The screen shows engine diagnostics; the real screens come in Phase 5.
Based on the openFPGA core template (https://github.com/open-fpga/core-template).
